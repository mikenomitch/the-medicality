How we do it

An embedded, measurable transformation.

We blend luxury hospitality playbooks with behavioral psychology to build a service-first culture that uplifts patients, employees, and ownership alike.

Strategic approach

Patient-first by design

  1. Apply proven ultra-luxury hotel methods and behavioral psychology to create a patient-first mindset.
  2. Secure buy-in from leadership through frontline staff to build a service culture that treats patients and employees as customers.
  3. Center on the principle that well-cared-for employees deliver superior service and performance.
  4. Serve ownership, patients, and employees equally well, measuring success by:
    • Ownership — asset value and profitability
    • Patients — satisfaction scores, impact on outcomes, and post-stay reviews
    • Employees — turnover rate and satisfaction survey scores
Tactical approach

Inside your operations, every day

  1. Embed within hospital operations with full access to understand current-state realities.
  2. Track patient satisfaction and emotional status alongside medical charts for a holistic view.
  3. Audit HR processes end to end to attract, train, motivate, and retain the right people.
  4. Coach leaders, clinicians, and service teams to model “service first” behaviors.
  5. Strengthen pre-arrival communication, in-hospital touchpoints, discharge, and post-stay follow-up.
  6. Respond immediately to patient and employee issues so concerns are resolved before discharge.
  7. Hold regular management huddles to map daily and weekly consultant activities.
  8. Host employee forums to surface improvements from those closest to care.
  9. Measure impact on profit, HCAHPS and Press Ganey scores, turnover, and employee satisfaction.
  10. Operate with compassion, patience, warmth, sensitivity, and visible care for the institution.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

About healthcare hospitality consulting and how we work with hospitals.

What does a healthcare hospitality consultant do?

We bring the systems, service standards, and operating protocols of ultra-luxury hotels into hospitals and clinics. That means designing every patient touchpoint — pre-arrival, arrival, the stay, discharge, and post-stay follow-up — so patients feel genuinely cared for, while giving staff the tools and support to deliver that service consistently.

How is Medicality different from a traditional patient experience consultancy?

We are proactive, not reactive. Instead of only reviewing survey data after discharge, we embed within your operations and put systems in place to resolve patient and employee issues while the patient is still in the building. Our team pairs a former President of Westin Hotels & Resorts North America with practicing physicians and clinical leaders.

How do you measure results?

We measure impact for all three constituencies we serve: ownership (asset value and profitability), patients (HCAHPS and Press Ganey scores, impact on outcomes, and post-stay reviews), and employees (turnover rate and satisfaction survey scores).

Why does employee experience matter for patient satisfaction?

Our approach is built on a principle proven in the finest hotels: well-cared-for employees deliver superior service. We audit HR processes end to end — how you attract, train, motivate, and retain people — and host employee forums so the people closest to care help shape the improvements.

Where do you work, and can we meet virtually?

We are based in Napa, California and partner with hospitals and health systems across the United States. We are delighted to meet in person or virtually — whichever is more comfortable for your team.

How do we get started?

Send us a note through the consultation form on this page. We will arrange an initial conversation to understand your goals, your current patient and employee experience, and whether an embedded engagement is the right fit.
